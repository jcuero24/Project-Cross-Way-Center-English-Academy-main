Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026.

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you browse this website or send a question through the contact form. The data controller is the legal operator of Cross Way Center; the operator should confirm and publish its full legal name, tax identification number (NIT), and legal address.

Information and purposes

The contact form asks for your name, email address, subject, and message. The testimonial form asks for your name, email address, program or level studied (optional), rating, and testimonial text. These details are used to respond to inquiries or review testimonial submissions. A testimonial may be published only after review by the academy and with the separate publication authorization requested in the form; submitting it does not publish it automatically. Do not include sensitive information, passwords, identity documents, or unnecessary personal data about someone else.

Contact form and service providers

The form is delivered through EmailJS, which receives the information you submit and transmits it to the recipient configured by the academy. This website does not use its own database to store form submissions. The provider, hosting service, and any optional services you accept may process technical data under their own terms, retention periods, and processing locations. The operator should review and document provider terms before operating the form.

Testimonials

Testimonial submissions are sent to the email channel configured by Cross Way Center for review by its team. Your name and testimonial text will be published only if the submission is approved and you checked the publication authorization. Your email address is used to manage the submission and is not displayed on the website with the testimonial.

Authorization

When sending the form, you must check the informed authorization box to allow your data to be used for the stated purpose. If a request is made by a minor, their legal representative must be involved and applicable enhanced protections must be followed.

Your rights and inquiries

You may request access, updates, correction, deletion, withdraw authorization, or file a complaint by writing to crosswaycentertulua@gmail.com. Requests will be handled under Colombia’s Law 1581 of 2012 and other applicable Colombian rules. You may also contact the Superintendence of Industry and Commerce after first following the required process with the data controller.

Security and international transfers

The site is served through Firebase Hosting using HTTPS. Reasonable technical safeguards are applied, but no transmission method is risk-free. EmailJS and optional third-party services may process information from other countries; review their policies before accepting optional services.

Updates

Changes will be published on this page with a revised update date.
